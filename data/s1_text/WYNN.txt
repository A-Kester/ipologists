pursuant to an agreement with the trustee for the second mortgage note holders. Pursuant to the terms of the disbursement agreement, we are required to use a substantial portion of the cash equity
contribution made to Wynn Las Vegas by Wynn Resorts before accessing the proceeds from the offering of the second mortgage notes. The disbursement agreement also requires us to use all of the proceeds

facilities or the FF&E facility until approximately 14-17 months after the closing of this offering and after applying all of the proceeds of this offering towards the development
