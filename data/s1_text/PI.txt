The RAIN market is relatively new and, to a large extent, unproven. RAIN technology and product adoption, including that of the Impinj Platform and Item Intelligence, will depend on numerous factors, including: • whether end users embrace the benefits we believe RAIN offers, and if so whether RAIN will achieve and sustain high demand and market adoption; • whether end users perceive that the benefits of RAIN adoption outweigh the cost and time to install or replace their existing systems and processes; and • whether the technological capabilities of RAIN products and applications meet end users’ current or anticipated needs.
